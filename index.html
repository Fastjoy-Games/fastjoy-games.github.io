FastJoy ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Honey Flow mobile Application collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Available online features vary by version and platform. When enabled or used, these features process the following information:

  • Player and account information. Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous player identifier. If you choose to link a supported platform account, we process the provider's account identifier to sign you in and recover progress. We do not receive your platform password.
  • Progress and social content. Cloud saves may include level progress, virtual currency, inventory, settings and purchase records. Leaderboards and teams may display your chosen player name, avatar, score and country/region. Team chat, membership, help requests and moderation reports are stored to operate these features. Do not share private information in public names or chat.
  • Usage and diagnostics. Firebase Analytics processes gameplay and interaction events, app/device identifiers and device information. Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports, diagnostic logs, app/device details and a player identifier to investigate errors. Firebase App Check processes app/device attestation signals to help prevent abuse.
  • Notifications and configuration. Firebase Cloud Messaging processes a notification token and related delivery information. Firebase Remote Config provides app configuration and may use app/device information to select settings.
  • Device and advertising identifiers. AppLovin MAX and enabled ad partners may process your advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID or, where permitted, IDFA), IP address, device model, operating system, language, approximate location and ad interactions to provide, measure and protect advertising.
  • Purchase information. Apple or Google processes payments. We receive product identifiers, transaction identifiers and receipts to deliver purchases, validate transactions, prevent replay and restore eligible purchases. We do not receive your payment card details.
  • Support requests. If you contact us, we receive your email address and information you provide so we can answer, verify account ownership and handle privacy or abuse reports.

We do not request access to your contacts, photos or precise device location. Basic play does not require you to provide an email address or link a platform account. Optional online features can still create an anonymous Firebase account.
