Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FastJoy Games ("FastJoy", "we", "us") handles personal information when you play Honey Flow (the "Game", package com.fastjoy.honeyflow) on Android. If you have questions, contact us at fastjoy.games@gmail.com.

1. Who is responsible for your data

FastJoy Games, the developer of the Game on Google Play, is the "controller" of the personal information processed for the Game under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD) and similar laws. We are based in Vietnam.

  • Privacy contact: fastjoy.games@gmail.com. Our postal address is available on request at this email.
  • We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer because we are not required to. The email above is our contact channel for data subjects, including for the purposes of the LGPD (canal de comunicação).

2. Summary

  • Honey Flow does not have user accounts. You cannot sign up or log in, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or password.
  • Your game progress (levels, coins, boosters, settings) is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers and there is no cloud save. If you uninstall the Game or clear its data, your progress is lost.
  • Purchases: the "No Ads" purchases are restored automatically when you reinstall the Game on a device signed in with the same Google account. Coins, bundles, packs, Bee Pass and other consumable items are added to your on-device progress and are lost if you uninstall the Game or clear its data, because we do not keep a copy of your progress.
  • The Game shows ads and uses analytics and crash reporting provided by third parties (Google AdMob and its mediation partners, Google Firebase). These services collect device and usage information as described below. You can limit this at any time (see section 4).

4. Advertising and your choices

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: when you first open the Game, a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform asks whether your data may be used for personalized ads, ad measurement and analytics. If you do not agree, you can still play; you will see non-personalized ads and analytics is limited. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the Game: Settings → Privacy. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before.
  • United States: residents of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon and other states with privacy laws can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information and of targeted advertising in the Game: open Settings → Privacy and choose not to allow the sale or sharing of your data. After you opt out, ads in the Game are limited to non-personalized ads.
  • Everywhere: you can delete or reset your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). If Settings → Privacy in the Game opens this web page instead of a privacy form, no in-game privacy form applies in your region; please use the Android setting, or contact us.
  • Ads in purchases: buying "No Ads" removes interstitial and banner ads. Rewarded ads remain available as an optional choice.

5. In-app purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases (for example coin packs, "No Ads", seasonal bundles and Bee Pass). All payments are processed by Google Play. We receive only the information Google Play provides to confirm a purchase and to restore the "No Ads" purchases (product and order details). We do not receive or store your payment card information.

  • Restored on reinstall: "No Ads" and the "No Ads" bundle's ad removal (one-time, non-consumable purchases), when you use the same Google account.
  • Not restored: coins, bundles, packs, offers and Bee Pass (consumable purchases). What you receive from them is stored in your on-device progress, so it is lost if you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

6. Legal bases for processing (EEA, UK, Switzerland, Brazil and similar laws)

  • Consent: personalized advertising and analytics in regions where consent is required. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy.
  • Performance of a contract: processing your in-app purchases, delivering the items you bought and restoring the "No Ads" purchases.
  • Legitimate interests: showing non-personalized ads to fund a free game, crash reporting and diagnostics to keep the Game working, remote settings to adjust the Game without an update, and fraud and abuse prevention. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests by contacting us.

Providing personal information is not a legal or contractual requirement. If you refuse consent you can still play the Game. We do not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you based solely on automated processing. Personalized advertising involves profiling by our ad partners, only where you allow it.

7. International data transfers

We are based in Vietnam, and our service providers (Google LLC, Unity Technologies and, when enabled, Meta Platforms, Inc.) process data in the United States and other countries. These countries may not offer the same level of protection as your country. Where required, transfers rely on the providers' safeguards, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and their UK and Brazilian equivalents).

For users in the Republic of Korea and Japan: personal information (Advertising ID, app instance and installation IDs, device information, IP address, in-game events, crash logs, purchase records) is transferred over the network while you use the Game, at the time of collection, to Google LLC (United States), Unity Technologies (United States) and, when enabled, Meta Platforms, Inc. (United States), for the purposes and retention periods described in this policy. These recipients are subject to the laws of the United States and maintain their own privacy and security programs. You can refuse by not giving consent where asked, by opting out in Settings → Privacy or in your Android ads settings, or by uninstalling the Game; core gameplay remains available.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Game progress is kept on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. We do not keep a copy.
  • Firebase Analytics event-level data is kept according to the data retention setting of our Firebase project (no longer than 14 months), then deleted. Aggregated reports that do not identify you may be kept longer.
  • Firebase Crashlytics crash reports are kept for a limited period according to Firebase's retention policy, then deleted.
  • Firebase Remote Config and Cloud Messaging identifiers are kept while the Game is installed and are deleted by Google after a period of inactivity, according to Firebase's settings.
  • Ad partners and Unity keep data according to their own retention policies (for Google, see https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention).
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play as required for payments, refunds and tax.

To request access to or deletion of data linked to your device, email fastjoy.games@gmail.com with your device's Advertising ID. Because we do not hold your name or contact details, we can only locate data through such identifiers; if you have deleted your Advertising ID, we may not be able to find data linked to your device. We will act on the request ourselves where we can (for example deleting Firebase Analytics data for that identifier) and forward it to the relevant providers. To stop future collection, you can also change your choice in Settings → Privacy, reset your Advertising ID or uninstall the Game.

9. Security

Information sent by the Game and the services above is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

10. Children and teenagers

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the minimum age set by the law of your country, for example 16 in some EU countries for consent to online services). If you are under the age at which you can consent to data processing in your country, please ask a parent or guardian to review this policy and your consent choices with you.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has used the Game in a way that allowed personal information to be collected, email fastjoy.games@gmail.com with the device's Advertising ID. We will ask our providers to delete the data associated with that identifier and take steps to stop further collection.

We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of users under 16 for cross-context behavioral advertising.
