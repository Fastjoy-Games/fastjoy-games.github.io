Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FastJoy ("we", "us") handles information when you play Honey Flow (the "Game") on Android. If you have questions, contact us at fastjoy.games@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • Honey Flow does not have user accounts. You cannot sign up or log in, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or password.
  • Your game progress (levels, coins, boosters, settings) is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers. If you uninstall the Game or clear its data, your progress is lost. Purchases made through Google Play are restored automatically when you reinstall.
  • The Game shows ads and uses analytics and crash reporting provided by third parties (Google AdMob and its mediation partners, Google Firebase). These services collect device and usage information as described below.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The Game itself does not run its own servers that store your personal data. The following third-party services, built into the Game, collect information when you play:

3. Advertising and your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): when you first open the Game, a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform asks whether ads may be personalized and whether data may be used for analytics. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy in the Game.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Ads in purchases: buying "No Ads" removes interstitial and banner ads. Rewarded ads remain available as an optional choice.

4. In-app purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases (for example coin packs, "No Ads", seasonal bundles and Bee Pass). All payments are processed by Google Play. We receive only the information Google Play provides to confirm and restore a purchase (product and order details). We do not receive or store your payment card information.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Game progress is kept on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data.
  • Data collected by the third-party services above is kept according to each provider's retention settings and policy.
  • Because the Game has no accounts, we do not hold data that is linked to your name or contact details. To limit future collection, you can reset your Advertising ID, opt out of personalized ads, change your consent choice in the Game, or uninstall the Game. For any privacy request, email fastjoy.games@gmail.com. Include your device's Advertising ID if you want us to forward a request to our providers.

6. Security

Information sent by the Game and the services above is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold account data, most requests are best handled through the controls above and through the providers listed in section 2. You may also contact us at fastjoy.games@gmail.com.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game changes. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version. Significant changes will be reflected in the Game's store listing.
