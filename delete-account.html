Nothing to delete on our side

You cannot sign up or log in to Honey Flow, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or password. This page previously described account deletion; the Game no longer has accounts, so there is no account or cloud profile to delete.

Your game progress

  • Your progress (levels, coins, boosters, settings) is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded to our servers.
  • Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data removes your progress. This is the closest equivalent to deleting your data — we hold no copy of it.
  • The "No Ads" purchases are restored automatically when you reinstall the Game with the same Google account. Coins, bundles, packs, Bee Pass and other consumable items are part of your on-device progress and are lost if you uninstall the Game or clear its data.
  • Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in the Game's settings or your Android notification settings.

Data from third-party services

The ads, analytics and crash-reporting services built into the Game (Google AdMob and its mediation partners, Google Firebase) collect device and usage information as described in our Privacy Policy. This data is held by those providers, not on our own servers. To limit collection you can:

  • change your ads consent choice, or opt out of the sale or sharing of your data (U.S. states), in the Game under Settings → Privacy;
  • reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads);
  • uninstall the Game.

Other privacy requests

For access, correction or deletion requests, or an abuse report, email fastjoy.games@gmail.com and describe your request. Include your device's Advertising ID so we can locate data linked to your device; we act on the request ourselves where we can and forward it to our providers.

Never send passwords, payment card numbers, authentication codes or account recovery codes. Sending an email starts a request; opening this page does not delete anything.
