Request account and data deletion

You can request deletion even if you no longer have the game installed or cannot sign in.

  1. In the game: open Settings and choose Delete Account where available, then review and confirm the deletion.
  2. By email: contact tommyphan772@gmail.com with the subject Honey Flow account deletion.

Include your player ID from your profile if available, your player name, Android or iOS, and whether you linked a platform account. If you cannot find your ID, explain what you can access so we can help identify the account. We may ask for limited information to verify ownership before deleting it.

Never send passwords, payment card numbers, authentication codes or account recovery codes. Sending an email starts a request; opening this page does not delete anything.

What happens to your data

  • We delete your game account, profile and cloud save, including saved progress and virtual inventory.
  • We remove team membership and leaderboard entries. Authored chat is removed or anonymized; team leadership may transfer so the team can continue.
  • We delete account-associated entitlement copies. Store payment records and purchases remain subject to Apple's or Google's policies. Account deletion does not request a store refund.
  • Limited transaction, fraud-prevention, security or support records may be retained when needed for disputes, abuse prevention or legal obligations. We will explain any exception and its retention period when responding.
  • Analytics, diagnostics, ad-provider records and backups may have separate retention schedules. Tell us if your request also concerns this data so we can help identify the applicable provider process.

We will review the request, verify ownership as needed, and confirm completion or explain any required retention. Contact the same email if you need help with a pending request.

Deletion can permanently remove progress. Uninstalling the app or resetting your advertising ID does not delete cloud data. If you keep the game installed, follow the confirmation instructions to clear local data; returning to online play can create a new anonymous account.

Need a different privacy request?

For access, correction, deletion of specific information, or an abuse report, email the same contact and describe your request. Read our Privacy Policy for the information processed by Honey Flow and our service providers.
